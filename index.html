Connect Google Sheet

This app saves data directly to your Google Sheet via an Apps Script Web App. Follow these steps once:

  1. Open your Google Sheet
  2. Click Extensions → Apps Script
  3. Delete the default code, paste the contents of Code.gs
  4. Click Deploy → New deployment
  5. Type: Web App · Execute as: Me · Who has access: Anyone
  6. Click Deploy, authorize, then copy the Web App URL
  7. Paste it below and click Save
